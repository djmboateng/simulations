About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at George Mason University and a frontend web developer. I have hands-on experience with AV systems, AI training, CAD tools, circuits and frontend web development, and I've been building websites since middle school. Growing up, I was also a big car enthusiast, which I love incorporating into my work.

I like making mission-driven projects and things people can actually play with. That's what this site is for.

Why simulations?

A simulation lets you change one thing and see what happens. I'm gathering the ones I build here so they're easy to find and play in any browser. Every simulation runs entirely on your device, and nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere.
