Opponent

This simulator is built around a low-poly 2000 Honda Civic Type R (EK9) that I designed in Onshape back in 10th grade. You can recolor it, lower it, widen it, bolt on wings and even a jet engine, then see how each change affects the car on the track.

The 2000 EK9 was a Japan-only 3-door hot hatch and the final year of the first-generation Type R.

Engine & Performance

  • B16B 1.6L inline-4, DOHC VTEC
  • 185 PS (182 hp) @ 8,200 rpm
  • 160 Nm (118 lb-ft) @ 7,500 rpm
  • 5-speed close-ratio manual, helical LSD
  • 0–60 mph ≈ 6.3 s · top speed ≈ 139 mph

Dimensions & Weight

  • Curb weight ≈ 1,050–1,070 kg
  • 4,180 × 1,695 × 1,360 mm
  • Wheelbase 2,620 mm
  • 195/55 R15 tires, 5-lug hubs

2000 Type R x Spec

  • Seam-welded high-rigidity chassis
  • Red bucket seats, MOMO wheel, aluminum shift knob
  • A/C, power folding mirrors, CD player
  • Championship White, Sparkle Silver, Starlight Black Pearl, Sunlight Yellow

The performance numbers are estimates from a simplified physics model, calibrated so the stock car matches the factory figures above.
